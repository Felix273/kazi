## Full Description (max 4000 chars)
**EN:**
Kazi is a hyperlocal job marketplace connecting employers and job seekers in Nairobi, Kenya.

**FOR JOB SEEKERS:**
- Find nearby jobs based on your location and skills
- Apply with one tap and get notified instantly
- Track your applications in real-time
- Get paid directly to your Kazi Wallet via M-Pesa
- Withdraw earnings anytime to your M-Pesa account

**FOR EMPLOYERS:**
- Post jobs in seconds with detailed requirements
- Find skilled workers near your location
- Manage applications and hire the best candidates
- Pay workers securely through integrated M-Pesa
- Boost your job listings for 3x more visibility

**KEY FEATURES:**
- 📍 GPS-based job matching for Nairobi neighborhoods
- 💬 Real-time chat between employers and workers
- 💰 Secure escrow payments via M-Pesa STK Push
- ⭐ Rating system for trust and accountability
- 🔒 Identity verification for all users
- 📊 Dashboard with job stats and analytics
- 🌍 Clear English interface
- 🌙 Dark mode for comfortable browsing
